## Connected apps

**Settings → Connected apps** on osint.ly lists your connections, grouped by workspace. For each workspace you see its plan and how many app slots are used.

For each app you see:

- when it was last used;
- the lookups it ran in the last 30 days;
- the credits it spent in the last 24 hours.

From this page you can:

- **Set a daily credit limit** for the app. See [Credits and limits](/assistants/credits-limits#daily-credit-limit-per-app).
- **Disconnect** the app. Its access stops immediately.

The page also shows the server URL and the setup steps for ChatGPT, Claude and Codex.

---

## History

Searches and lookups started from an assistant appear in your [history](/guides/history) with a badge: **Started from ChatGPT**, **Claude** or **Codex**. Open them like any other result.

---

## Organizations

In an organization workspace, the assistant uses the organization's plan, limits and shared credits, and its searches appear in the organization's history.

Owners and admins control assistants in **Organization settings**, in the **ChatGPT & Claude** section:

- turn assistants on or off for the whole organization;
- see which members connected an app, when it was last used and its activity;
- disconnect any member's app.

Turning assistants off disconnects every member and blocks new connections to the organization. A member who leaves the organization loses access through their assistant at the next request.

With single sign-on (SSO), an assistant can connect only to the organization linked to your SSO sign-in.
