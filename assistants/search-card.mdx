In ChatGPT and Claude, a full search appears as an Osintly card inside the assistant's answer. The card follows the host's light or dark theme and links to the full report on osint.ly.

Codex shows the card only where the app supports interactive MCP Apps. Elsewhere, the assistant gives the same progress and findings as text, with a link to the full report, and the card controls below (**Show more**, follow-up buttons) are not shown.

---

## Live progress

As soon as the search starts, the card shows the target and its type, then follows the search live:

- a status line with the latest module that returned a result;
- the time elapsed and the number of module results;
- the logos of the services found, as they arrive.

When the search finishes, the card shows **Search complete** with its duration and totals. Select **Open the full report** to view it on osint.ly.

If the same target was searched in the last 7 days, the assistant reopens that search instead of paying again. The card then reads **Reopened the search from** with its date, and **no credit used**.

---

## Findings

Once the search is complete, the assistant presents the findings: a short sentence, then the Osintly section it introduces.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Module results** | The profiles the modules found, with their main fields. **Open** opens the profile on its site. |
| **Accounts found** | The services where an account exists for an email address. |
| **Known breaches** | The breaches that contain the target, with their date. |
| **Follow-ups** | The next lookups worth running. |

In the card, long lists show the first items: select **Show more** to expand them. A section with nothing to show is left out, and the assistant says so in its answer.

The assistant then gives its analysis: what the findings support, how confident it is and the next step.

<Warning>
A registered account or a matching username is a lead, not proof of identity. Check the full report before you rely on a finding.
</Warning>

---

## Follow-ups

Follow-ups come from the search itself: a username found on a profile, an email address, a website. They are ranked by how many modules confirm them, and each one shows what it costs:

- **IP lookup**, **Domain lookup** or **Web archives · tool limit**: a focused lookup, within your tool limits;
- **Full search · 1 credit**: a new full search;
- **Searched … · free**: a target searched in the last 7 days, reopened at no cost.

In the card, select a follow-up to ask the assistant to run it. Without the card, ask for it in the conversation. A full search you did not request always needs your confirmation first.

---

## Comparing two searches

When two searches may concern the same person, ask the assistant to compare them. The card lists the identifiers they share:

- **Same account ID**: the same platform identifier, such as a Ubisoft or Xbox ID;
- **Same email**;
- **Same username**.

An identical ID or email links the accounts in the data. It does not show who controls them.
