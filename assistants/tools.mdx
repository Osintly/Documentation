The assistant picks the right tool from your request: you do not need to name it. This page lists every tool so you know what to ask for.

<Note>
Focused lookups count toward your tool limits. Only full searches use credits. See [Credits and limits](/assistants/credits-limits).
</Note>

---

## Your workspace

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Account** | Shows the connected workspace, its plan, the credit balance, the lookups left today and the daily credit limit you set for this app. | Free |
| **History** | Lists the workspace's recent full searches, or the saved results of one tool. | Free |
| **Saved result** | Reopens a saved tool result from the history, without running the lookup again. | Free |

---

## Focused lookups

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **IP intelligence** | Geolocation, network owner, BGP, WHOIS, reverse DNS, risk, open ports, reputation and hosted domains for one IP address. An IP location is approximate and never a person's address. |
| **Domain intelligence** | DNS, HTTP, TLS certificates, technologies, WHOIS, security headers, risk, subdomains, emails, ports and reputation for one domain. |
| **Web archives** | The years in which a page of a domain was archived, then the snapshots of one year. |
| **Platform accounts** | One account on Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Epic Games, Roblox, Rockstar Social Club, Ubisoft or Reddit: profile, linked accounts, games and activity, depending on the platform. |
| **Vehicle history** | A US VIN or a French license plate: vehicle identity, history signals and recalls. |

Each lookup is saved in your history and counts toward your [tool limits](/assistants/credits-limits#tool-limits). The assistant links to the saved result on osint.ly.

<Tip>
A matching username on two platforms does not prove that both accounts belong to the same person. The assistant treats it as a lead.
</Tip>

## Radar

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Radar breaches** | Browses the publicly disclosed breaches indexed by [Osintly Radar](/breach-intelligence/radar), filtered by severity or exposure and sorted by date or size. | Free |
| **Radar breach** | Reads the details of one breach. | Free |

Breach details describe the incident. They do not show whether a specific person was exposed: a full search does that.

---

## Full searches

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Start a full search** | Searches a pseudonym, email address, IP address, domain or crypto wallet across all Osintly modules, account checks and known breaches. | 1 credit |
| **Prepare a full search** | Checks the cost and your balance before the assistant suggests a search you did not ask for. | Free |
| **Search status and results** | Follow a running search and read its results. | Free |
| **Present results** | Shows the findings in the [search card](/assistants/search-card) with the assistant's explanation. | Free |
| **Compare searches** | Shows the account IDs, emails and usernames two searches have in common. | Free |

The search types available follow your plan. Leaked data stays in the full report on osint.ly: the assistant only sees which breaches contain the target.
