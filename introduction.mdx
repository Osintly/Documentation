## What is Osintly?

[Osintly](https://osint.ly) is a unified <Tooltip tip="Open Source Intelligence - collecting and analysing publicly available information">OSINT</Tooltip> platform. Search across pseudonyms, emails, domains, IPs, and crypto addresses, or use focused intelligence tools. Results stream in as providers respond. Limited pseudonym and email previews are available [for free](/guides/free-searches).
