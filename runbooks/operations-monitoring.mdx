---
title: Monitoring runbook
description: Check scheduled monitors, alerts, and notification delivery.
icon: bell
---

1. Select the Personal or organization workspace that owns the monitor, then open [Monitoring & Alerts](https://osint.ly/monitoring).
2. In **Monitors**, confirm that the target, check type, frequency, and status match the intended investigation. Check the latest run time; displayed dates and times are UTC.
3. In **Alerts**, review new findings, severity, and workflow status. Verify significant findings with a fresh search or source record before acting.
4. In **Channels**, confirm the email or Discord destination for this workspace. Organization owners and admins manage channels.
5. If a scheduled check did not run, verify the workspace plan, monitor limit, remaining credits, and role. Capture the monitor ID, the UTC time of the expected run, and any error before contacting [support](https://osint.ly/support).

See [Monitoring & Alerts](/operations/monitoring) for the user-facing workflow.
