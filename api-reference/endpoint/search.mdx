<Warning>
`Domain Name` and `IP Address` still work in this endpoint, but they will soon stop being supported in the Search API. Their replacements for new lookups are [Domain Intelligence](/tools-reference/domain-intelligence) and [IP Intelligence](/tools-reference/ip-intelligence). No cutoff date has been announced. The website's free previews are not available through this endpoint.
</Warning>
