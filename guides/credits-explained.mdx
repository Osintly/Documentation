Full app searches cost credits. Limited [free search previews](/guides/free-searches) use their own quota and never deduct a credit. The selected workspace determines which balance is used.

<Warning>
The selected workspace needs an active subscription to spend credits. In Personal, that means your own subscription. In an organization, it means the organization subscription.
</Warning>

---

## Personal credits

One credit pays for one full search query, including a fresh rerun of an existing query.

| Personal plan | Price | Credits per month |
|---|---:|---:|
| Standard | €9.99 | 25 |
| Pro | €24.99 | 100 |
| Ultimate | €94.99 | 200 |

Ultimate is the current name for the highest tier. Existing Advanced subscriptions receive the corresponding Ultimate access.

Purchased credits do not expire. Promotional or free credits can have an expiration date stated in the applicable promotion terms.

---

## What uses credits

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="New full searches" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Each new full search uses one credit in the workspace that is selected when you launch it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Rerunning a query" icon="rotate-right">
    A rerun is a new execution to retrieve fresh data, so it also uses one credit. Opening a saved result does not.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Eligible tool-limit overrides" icon="wrench">
    When a focused tool lookup hits a five-minute, hourly, or daily volume limit, Osintly may offer **Use 1 credit**. You must confirm the charge. Cooldowns and concurrent-request limits cannot be bypassed with a credit.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What does not use credits

- Reopening a search or saved tool result from history
- Exporting an existing result
- AI Analyst conversations on an existing result
- Limited free previews

## Organization credits

Organizations have their own shared credit allowance, separate from the balances of their members.

| Organization plan | Credits per paid seat |
|---|---:|
| Pro | 100 |
| Ultimate | 200 |

The allowance equals the credits per seat multiplied by the number of paid seats. For example, a three-seat Pro organization has 300 shared credits in its billing period. Members do not receive fixed individual portions: any member can consume the shared allowance.

Osintly reserves an organization credit before starting a full search and settles it only when the search is created. If launch fails, the reservation is released. An eligible tool-limit override uses the same shared allowance after confirmation.

Personal credits are never used automatically for an organization action, and organization credits are never used in Personal. Legacy Team workspaces retain their own historical credit behavior.

---

## Check usage and manage a balance

- In **Personal**, use the sidebar, Search page, or **Settings → Credits** to check your balance and history.
- In an **organization**, use the workspace context for the current allowance and **Organization → Usage** to review shared consumption and member activity.
- To change a personal plan, use **Settings → Billing**. Organization owners manage organization plans, seats, invoices, and payment method in **Organization → Billing**.

If a usable balance reaches zero, new full searches and eligible credit overrides are unavailable until the allowance is restored. Saved history stays accessible.
