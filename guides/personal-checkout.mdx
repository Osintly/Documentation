The [pricing page](https://osint.ly/pricing) separates **Personal**, **Organizations**, and **API** plans. These are distinct subscriptions. Select the audience that matches the workspace you want to fund before starting checkout.

## Personal purchase

1. Choose Standard, Pro, or Ultimate from the Personal plans.
2. Sign in or create an account if requested. The checkout flow can return you to the selected plan after authentication or MFA.
3. Choose the offered payment method. Card and other supported Stripe methods use the embedded checkout; cryptocurrency checkout is a separate path and currently supports monthly billing only.
4. Review the plan and payment details before confirming. The success page may show a processing state while payment and subscription access are being finalized.

Individual plans do not include Investigations. Older Personal and Team investigations remain available for [ZIP export](/collaboration/investigation-projects), while new shared investigations require an Organization plan.

The listed monthly Personal prices and included credits are in [Credits & subscriptions](/billing/credits-subscriptions). Payment methods, taxes, and the amount due are shown at checkout.

## Change or cancel a plan

Open **Settings → Billing** to review your current Personal subscription, invoices, and available plan changes. The interface shows whether a change takes effect immediately or at the next billing cycle before confirmation. A cancellation remains effective through the current paid period. Organization owners manage organization plans and seats in **Organization → Billing**; API plans use the separate [API workspace](/api-reference/api-plans-capacity).

Dates and clock times shown in Osintly billing screens are displayed in **UTC**.
